The Annex

A restored one-bedroom wing with its own front door: open-plan living room and kitchen, a quiet bedroom at the back, an en-suite shower room and a private patio.

Living room and kitchen

One open room under a high Victorian ceiling, with a skylight high above the hall end. Sofa in the bay, wingback by the fire, and a breakfast bar for three on the island.

Bedroom

Quiet and at the back of the house. A cane-headed European king-size bed, wider than a UK king, with soft bedding, bedside lamps, and shutters for proper darkness.

Shower room

En-suite, with a level-access rainfall shower and built-in bench, a heated towel rail, and a backlit mirror with demister.

Patio

Your own patio, for your exclusive use, with a table and two chairs. Tucked against the old garden wall, it's the spot for morning coffee or a drink after a swim.
