Edinburgh

Scotland's famous capital, on Portobello's doorstep. Castle, closes and cobbles, galleries and museums, and some of the world's biggest festivals.

The heart of Edinburgh is 3 miles away, 7 minutes by train. Spend the day in the city, then come home to the sea.

Train, bus or taxi

By train

Brunstane station, a 10-minute walk away, is your train into Edinburgh. It's one stop and 7 minutes to Edinburgh Waverley, in the heart of the city, with the Old Town on one side and the New Town on the other. The same line runs south to the Scottish Borders.

By bus

The 26 and 19 both stop at the top of Brunstane Road North, a couple of minutes' walk. The 26 reaches Princes Street in about 25 minutes, with buses roughly every 10 minutes, and the 19 takes a different route into the city centre, also stopping by Waverley. The N26 runs through the night, so there's no rush to leave the Festival or Hogmanay.

By taxi

The city centre and Leith are just a short drive away by taxi or ride-hailing app. Expect to pay from about £15, depending on demand.
